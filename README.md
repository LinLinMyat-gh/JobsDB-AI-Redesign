# Redesigning Jobsdb Thailand by using AI
- Explore the redesigned result here: https://linlinmyat-gh.github.io/JobsDB-AI-Redesign/

## 1. Introduction

Jobsdb is Thailand's leading employment marketplace. Despite its dominance, the platform has a critical gap. It treats all users the same, ignoring the distinct needs of foreign job seekers.

As a foreigner who use Jobsdb, I usually browse **100 job postings** daily only to find **5-8 relevant ones**. Job descriptions in Thai, vague language requirements, and no clear indication of whether companies hire foreigners made the process time-consuming and inefficient. This redesign addresses these pain points through simple segmentation.

---

## 2. Current Website Analysis

**Strengths:**
- Extensive job listings across all industries
- AI-powered job recommendations
- Mobile accessibility
- Salary insights and benchmarking

**Weaknesses for Foreign Users:**
- No segmentation between local and foreign-targeted jobs
- Ambiguous employer preferences (Thai nationals only vs. open to foreigners)
- Inconsistent job description languages

---

## 3. Problems Identified

Foreigners must open each job posting individually to check eligibility, which is highly inefficient. Thai descriptions are inaccessible to non-Thai speakers, while English postings may still require Thai proficiency. Companies do not signal whether they welcome foreign applicants, and 90% or more of opened jobs are irrelevant for foreigners.

---

## 4. Features Missing

| Missing Feature | Impact |
|-----------------|--------|
| Separate foreign job search | Foreigners waste hours on irrelevant postings |
| Language requirement filter | Mismatched applications waste everyone's time |
| "Open to Foreigners" badges | Candidates can't quickly assess eligibility |
| Segmented landing experience | Neither user group gets an optimized experience |

---

## 5. AI-Powered Improvements

### 1. Dual-Track Search Interface
Separate **"Local Jobs"** and **"Jobs for Foreigners"** tabs. Foreigners see only positions explicitly open to them.

### 2. Smart Nationality Detection
AI scans job postings for phrases like "Thai nationality required" or "open to foreigners" and tags them automatically.

### 4. Personalized Foreigner Dashboard
Shows visa guidance, work permit info, and curated resources for international professionals.

## 7. Conclusion

**User Experience Improvements:**

The redesign significantly reduces screening time from hours to minutes for foreign job seekers. Job matches become more accurate, leading to higher application success rates. Clear employer preferences help reduce confusion, and the platform becomes truly inclusive for all users.

**Business Outcomes:**

The platform will experience higher user retention among expatriates living in Thailand. The user base will expand to include Thailand's growing foreign community. Employers will see better satisfaction as they receive more qualified applicants for their positions. Jobsdb will achieve competitive differentiation through inclusivity, standing out from other job platforms in the market.

Jobsdb already champions fair hiring through its **"Fair Hiring"** initiative. This redesign doesn't create barriers—it removes them, helping qualified candidates find positions where their skills are valued.
